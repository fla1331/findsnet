What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your actions and preferences over time.

Which Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Necessary for the basic functioning of the site. They cannot be disabled.

  • Theme preference (light/dark)
  • Cookie consent

Performance and Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors interact with the site by collecting anonymous information.

  • Most visited pages
  • Time spent on the site
  • Traffic source

Functionality Cookies

Allow the site to remember your choices (e.g., selected language) to provide a more personalized experience.

Marketing Cookies

Used to display ads and deals more relevant to you and your interests. They may be placed by third parties, such as affiliate platforms.

How to Manage Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Accept or reject through our cookie banner
  • Browser settings: most browsers allow you to accept, reject, or delete cookies
  • Clear existing cookies at any time

By disabling cookies, some features of the site may not work properly.
