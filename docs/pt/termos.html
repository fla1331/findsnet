About Finds

Information about the project

About

Finds is a blog about deals, coupons and products that are actually worth it. Started in 2026 as a personal project to share finds and promotions without the gimmicks.

There's no team or big structure — just one person researching, testing and publishing. The idea is simple: show what's worth it, no fluff.

What is published

  • Deals and promotions that are actually worth it.
  • Coupons that work — tested before publishing.
  • Products we actually use and recommend.
